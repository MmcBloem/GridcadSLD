Files you control

Drawing work stays local.

The app processes drawing geometry on your device. Save projects locally or use locations such as iCloud Drive through the system file picker.

How your information is handled →

Free to start. Optional Pro unlock.

The free app has no time limit. An optional one-time purchase unlocks Pro features, including Library package transfer. Purchases and restoration use Apple’s App Store.

Features and compatibility depend on the app version and device. Calculator results support your workflow and require independent verification.
