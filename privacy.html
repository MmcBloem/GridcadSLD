Privacy policy

How gridCAD SLD and this support website handle information.

Updated 13 October 2026 · Developer: Michael McIntyre

Summary

gridCAD SLD is an offline-first drawing application developed by Michael McIntyre. The developer does not operate user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or profiling services for the app. Drawings are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Optional Pro purchases and restoration are handled through Apple’s App Store.

Drawings and files

Drawings, imported and exported files, reusable libraries, custom calculator files and preferences are stored on your device or in locations you select through the system file picker. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles storage and syncing under its own terms. You control saved files by moving, exporting or deleting them. GridCAD also keeps bounded automatic recovery copies on the device, excluded from backup. Use Recover Drawings to open or delete closed recovery copies, and save recovered work to Files for a durable copy.

Diagnostics and recovery

The app keeps a small rolling action history and session marker on the device, excluded from backup. These record operation names, short internal identifiers, counts and technical status, rather than drawing contents, drawing names or file paths. Reports are exported only when you request them. Clear Diagnostic History removes diagnostic logs without deleting drawings or recovery copies. Device-level crash reporting and diagnostic sharing are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Camera and photos

Camera access is requested when you choose Take Picture. Photo selection is used when you choose images to insert. Selected images are processed on-device and become part of the drawing you control. You can revoke access in device Settings. Core drawing features remain available without camera or photo permission.

Clipboard and processing

The app uses the system clipboard for copy, cut, paste and export actions. Clipboard content is not transmitted to the developer. Drawing geometry is processed on-device. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

Purchases

The free app has no time limit. An optional one-time non-consumable purchase unlocks Pro. Apple processes payment and manages purchase history through your Apple Account. GridCAD uses StoreKit on-device to verify access and restore purchases. The developer does not receive payment-card details or upload transaction information to a developer-operated server. Apple handles its services under the App Store privacy information and Apple Privacy Policy.

Information you send to support

If you email mikegencor1@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send confidential content unless necessary. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. Email providers process correspondence under their own policies.

This website and GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The supplied website contains no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded third-party media, contact forms or cookies set by its own code. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or using email takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Delete saved drawings and exports from their locations using Files or Finder. Deleting the app removes its app-private data and preferences, subject to device backups managed by Apple. Files saved outside the app’s private storage can remain and must be deleted separately. App Store purchase history is managed by Apple and is not removed by uninstalling the app. There is no developer-operated remote app account to delete.

Children

GridCAD SLD is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as app functionality or requirements change. The published revision date will be updated on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact Michael McIntyre at mikegencor1@gmail.com.
